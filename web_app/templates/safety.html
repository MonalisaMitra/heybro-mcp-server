    Before you go — a quick checklist

    • Share your route and expected check-in times with someone who isn't travelling with you.
    • Save the local emergency number and your embassy/consulate contact offline (screenshot or write it down).
    • Check the official advisory for your destination and nationality — not just the forecast.
    • Carry a physical map or save offline maps; don't rely on a signal that may not be there.
    • Know the nearest hospital and the fastest way to reach it from where you're staying.
    • If a GDACS or official alert is active near your route, have a fallback plan and a later decision point.
    • Keep devices charged and carry a backup power source on remote legs of a trip.
